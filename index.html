Calendar

Your calendar builds your to-do list.

Start any event's title with PUREFOCUS: and it appears in your task list. Works with Google Calendar and more.

  • Automatic. New events show up within a couple of minutes, and the event's length sets the number of pomodoros.
  • Type and location included. Add Type: Study and Location: Library to the description.
  • Canvas assignments too. Set a type once and it carries across that course.
  • Read-only. Pure Focus never changes your calendars.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    The short version: it's free, it's private, and it takes about a minute to start.

    Is Pure Focus free?

    Yes. Create a free account, open the app and press Space. No payment, no ads.

    Where is my data stored?

    In your Pure Focus account, so it follows you to every device. Your browser also keeps a copy for fast, offline use. You can export everything to CSV, and import it back, at any time.

    Does it work with Canvas?

    Yes. Add your Canvas calendar link and assignments show up on the calendar and in Today's Tasks, with their descriptions. Blackbaud, Outlook, Apple and any other .ics calendar link work too. Calendar links need the small Pure Focus helper or a hosted version of the app.

    How do I turn a calendar event into a task?

    Start its title with PUREFOCUS: (capitals don't matter). Add Type: and Location: lines to the description and the task picks those up as well. It works from Google Calendar or any calendar you've connected.

    Which browsers does it work in?

    Current versions of Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox, on computers and phones. The Focus Guard site blocker is a Chromium extension, so it runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc; in Safari, the Mac app does that job.

    What can Google sign-in see?

    Signing in to Pure Focus with Google only shares your name, email address and profile photo, so your account and sync work. Connecting Google Calendar or Google Tasks is a separate, optional step that asks for read-only access, so Pure Focus can show your events and tasks but can't create, change or delete anything in your Google account. Prefer not to connect? Import an .ics file instead.

    Does it work offline?

    While the app is open, your timer, tasks and reports keep working without a connection, and changes sync the next time you're online. Signing in, streaming scenes and Google import need internet.

    Are there keyboard shortcuts?

    Press Space to start or pause the timer. While editing a task, ⌘ ↩ (Ctrl Enter on Windows) saves it and Esc closes it.

    What is the Pomodoro Technique?

    A simple rhythm: work for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, and after every fourth round take a longer 15-minute break. Pure Focus lets you change every length and the long-break interval.
