    What earns XP?
    • Focus time10 XP a minute for your first 5 hours a day, then 5 XP a minute up to 8 hours
    • Finishing a session2 XP per minute of the session, up to 100
    • Rating a session20 XP, whatever the rating
    • Your daily goal2 XP per goal minute when you reach it
    • Quests40 · 70 · 120 XP, and 70 more for all three
    • Streaks and combosUp to +25 % for a streak and +20 % for back-to-back sessions
    • Golden sessionsOne finished session in ten doubles its minute XP

    XP is worked out from your session history, so it is the same on every device.
